# Brag Plan: UNICAL CS HUB

## What is this app?
UNICAL CS HUB is the premier all-in-one digital academic portal built specifically for 100-level Computer Science students at the University of Calabar (UNICAL), centralizing lecture notes, weekly lecture timetables, coursework assignments, and official departmental communication in one unified, modern interface.

## The angle
A cinematic, Silicon Valley-grade SaaS product launch tailored for Nigerian university students. Academic life for 100L university students is notoriously fragmented—scattered WhatsApp groups, misplaced PDF notes, sudden venue changes, and missed assignment deadlines. UNICAL CS HUB solves this with one modern, high-performance platform.

## Hook (first 2-3 seconds)
High-contrast deep navy void with an ambient royal blue glow. A glowing graduation cap emblem materializes with the bold typographic hook:
**"Introducing UNICAL CS HUB."**
Subtle bass drop transitions to:
**"Your Academic Life. One Platform."**

## Key moments (the middle)
1. **The Student Dashboard (Scene 2):** High-polish overview card with 100L Computer Science badge, quick metric pills, active courses banner, and announcement notifications.
2. **Course Notes Library (Scene 3):** Clean visual display of all official 100L Computer Science courses (COS 101, CSC 181, GST 111, MTH 101, PHY 101, STA 111) with downloadable lecture notes badges and topic tags.
3. **Weekly Lecture Timetable (Scene 4):** Crisp day-by-day timetable matrix showing time slots, course codes, lecturer assignments, and real campus lecture hall venues (New Science Lecture Theatre, Lab 1).
4. **Assignments & Deadlines (Scene 5):** Academic continuous assessment cards featuring instructions, max scores (10-30 Marks), and countdown deadlines ("Due: In 3 Days").
5. **Seamless Navigation (Scene 6):** Rapid-fire UI transition showcasing smooth switching between Notes, Timetable, Tasks, and Course Access.

## Outro / punchline
The camera pulls back to reveal the official brand emblem, university departmental identity, and the permanent call-to-action:
**UNICAL CS HUB**
*"Your Department. Your Resources. Your Academic Advantage."*
**Website: https://unicalcshub.netlify.app/**

## Storyboard

### Scene 1 — The Hook (0.0s – 3.5s)
- **Visual:** Deep navy background with an ambient radial blue gradient. Glowing logo box with graduation cap icon scales smoothly into view.
- **Text:** "Introducing UNICAL CS HUB" fades in with tracking expansion, then shifts into "Your Academic Life. One Platform."
- **Audio:** Low cinematic riser, subtle impact on logo lockup, clean synth groove begins.
- **Transition:** Cinematic crossfade to Scene 2.

### Scene 2 — The Student Dashboard (3.5s – 7.2s)
- **Visual:** Clean presentation of the UNICAL CS HUB interface. Top navigation bar with "100L Department of Computer Science", student welcome header ("Welcome back, Scholar"), quick stat badges (7 Courses Enrolled, 24 Lecture Notes, 100% Verified Access).
- **Audio:** Driving drum beat kicks in, subtle interface click sound.
- **Transition:** Smooth lateral slide into Scene 3.

### Scene 3 — Course Notes & Materials (7.2s – 11.0s)
- **Visual:** "Access Your Lecture Notes." Showcase of 100L course cards: COS 101 (Intro to CS), CSC 181 (Computer Applications), GST 111 (Use of English), MTH 101 (Elementary Mathematics), PHY 101 (General Physics), and STA 111. PDF lecture notes download badges glowing in royal blue.
- **Audio:** Syncopated percussion, soft paper/material reveal sound.
- **Transition:** Smooth crossfade to Scene 4.

### Scene 4 — Weekly Lecture Timetable (11.0s – 14.8s)
- **Visual:** "Stay Ahead With Your Timetable." High-definition schedule table displaying Monday to Friday slots, course codes, venue allocations (New Science Lecture Theatre, Software Lab), and assigned lecturers.
- **Audio:** Energetic synth melody, subtle digital chime.
- **Transition:** Smooth wipe to Scene 5.

### Scene 5 — Coursework & Assignments (14.8s – 18.5s)
- **Visual:** "Never Miss An Assignment." Active continuous assessment cards showing course codes, assignment titles, instructions, score badges ("20 Marks", "30 Marks"), and deadline countdown timers.
- **Audio:** Bass drop accent, tick-down sound effect.
- **Transition:** Dynamic zoom transition to Scene 6.

### Scene 6 — Unified Experience (18.5s – 21.5s)
- **Visual:** "Everything You Need For Your Academic Journey." Fast-paced micro-switch between Notes, Timetable, Tasks, and Support, emphasizing that everything is instant and in one place.
- **Audio:** Peak musical energy with fast rhythmic hi-hats.
- **Transition:** Flash/dip transition to Scene 7.

### Scene 7 — Outro & Grand Finale (21.5s – 24.5s)
- **Visual:** Premium closing screen. Large central UNICAL CS HUB emblem with ambient glowing halo.
- **Text:**
  - UNICAL CS HUB
  - "Your Department. Your Resources. Your Academic Advantage."
  - Website: https://unicalcshub.netlify.app/
- **Audio:** Music reaches its triumphant resolution and fades with a warm atmospheric tail.
